What you get

Labels that don't wear off

Your labels are part of the plate itself, not printed on top of it. Nothing to peel at the corners, smudge under a wet boot, or fade out after a season of gigging.

White on matte black is the standard combination — readable under stage wash, and legible at standing height.

Clips on. Nothing to unscrew.

The plate drops over the switches and grips the side edges of the chassis. No adhesive, no screws, no disassembly — and it comes off just as easily when you want to change your layout.

Cutouts clear the screen, the encoder, and every switch collar. The LED rings stay fully visible — nothing on the pedal is covered, dimmed, or harder to reach than it was before.

Made to order

Every plate is made to order with your labels — nothing is pulled off a shelf and nothing is pre-printed.

Black with white labels is the standard. Custom colours are available on request, so ask before you order and it can usually be matched.

  • Finish Matte black with a fine surface texture
  • Labels White, uppercase, formed into the plate
  • Colours Black with white labels as standard; others on request
  • Fitting Snap-on side clips — no tools, no adhesive
  • STD 10 Ships in two halves that join with a dovetail; the pedal is wider than can be made in one piece, and the seam sits between the two right-hand switches
  • In the box One faceplate. Your pedal, switches, and hardware are untouched

Your labels

No two rigs are labelled the same way, so there is no standard set. You say what goes on each switch, and that's the plate you get.

Order first, then send your layout — a reply goes out with the order asking for it. Nothing is made until your labels are confirmed, so take your time getting them right.

Ask a question first

  • Nano 4 Up to 4 labels
  • Mini 6 Up to 6 labels
  • STD 10 Up to 10 labels
  • Length Roughly 10 characters per label before the type gets too small to read on stage — shorter is better
  • Not sure yet Send your MIDI setup and a layout can be suggested
