Why MAX?

🔁

Bidirectional MIDI

Your DAW or plugin host can drive the device's LEDs and display — not just listen to button presses. State on stage, always in sync.

🎛️

Visual config editor

Labels, CC numbers, colors, short/long press actions — all edited in a desktop app for Mac, Windows, and Linux. No JSON required.

🔓

Open & hackable

CircuitPython firmware with full source on GitHub, documented hardware internals, and one-click install straight from the editor.

One switch, two jobs

VERB
reverb off · shimmer off

Tap = reverb on/off. Hold ½ second = shimmer mode.

Keytimes mode — a MAX exclusive

Every switch can run two independent message cycles: one for short presses, one for long. Here a tap works the reverb (CC 20, white LED) while a half-second hold flips shimmer on top of it (CC 21, magenta).

With long_overlay, the shimmer color stays parked on the LED as a mode indicator while taps keep cycling the reverb underneath — one glance tells you both states. Stock firmware gives you one action per switch; MAX gives every switch a short cycle, a long cycle, and an LED that never lies.
